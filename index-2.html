HOW BETLINK WORKS

Four steps, start to settlement

  1. 01

    Create a bet

    Pick an event, choose your side, set your stake in virtual credits.

  2. 02

    Someone takes the other side

    Another user reviews the terms and accepts the opposite outcome.

  3. 03

    BetLink locks the stakes

    Both stakes are held safely until the event finishes.

  4. 04

    Winner gets paid

    The result settles the bet automatically, minus a small platform fee.

This is a prototype

Every credit, balance, and payout on BetLink is virtual and exists only to demonstrate how a peer-to-peer betting marketplace could work. No real money is deposited, wagered, or withdrawn at any point in this version.
